Privacy Policy

How MAYNA collects, uses and protects information when you use our platform.

Last updated: 20 September 2026

1. Introduction

MAYNA is a business discovery and listing platform designed to help people find businesses and services, including businesses operating in Namibia

This Privacy Policy explains what information MAYNA may collect, why we collect it, how it may be used, and the choices available to users and business owners.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use MAYNA, we may collect information such as:

  • Business names and descriptions
  • Business categories and services
  • Business locations and towns
  • Phone numbers and email addresses
  • Business images and gallery content
  • Business operating hours
  • Information submitted through registration, login, support or contact forms
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure and maintain the platform

3. How We Use Information

MAYNA may use collected information to:

  • Create and display business listings
  • Allow users to discover businesses and services
  • Allow business owners to manage their listings
  • Respond to support requests and reports
  • Verify, review and moderate listings
  • Protect MAYNA against abuse, fraud, spam and security threats
  • Maintain and improve the platform

4. Public Business Information

MAYNA is a public business directory. Information that a business submits for its public listing may be visible to anyone using the platform.

This may include the business name, category, description, location, contact information, services, opening hours and approved images.

Business owners should avoid submitting private information that they do not want displayed publicly.

5. Account and Security Information

Certain information used for authentication and account security is kept private and is not intended to appear in public business listings.

MAYNA uses reasonable technical measures to protect account information and restrict unauthorized access to administrative and business-owner functions.

6. Contact and Support Messages

When you contact MAYNA through the Contact & Support page, we may store your name, email address, subject and message so that we can review and respond to your request.

7. Images and Uploaded Content

Businesses may be able to upload images for their listings. Uploaded content may be stored using third-party infrastructure or cloud services used by MAYNA.

Users should only upload content that they have permission to use and publish.

8. Service Providers

MAYNA may rely on third-party service providers for services such as website hosting, database storage, image hosting and email delivery.

These providers may process information when necessary to provide their services to MAYNA.

9. Cookies and Sessions

MAYNA may use cookies or similar session technologies where necessary for features such as administrator and business-owner authentication.

These technologies help keep users signed in securely and support essential platform functionality.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide MAYNA's services, maintain business listings, handle support requests, meet legal obligations and protect the platform.

11. Your Choices

Business owners may contact MAYNA regarding their information, including requests to correct or remove information associated with their business listing.

Requests may need to be verified before changes are made in order to protect business owners and platform users.

12. Children's Privacy

MAYNA is intended as a business and service discovery platform and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to This Policy

MAYNA may update this Privacy Policy as the platform develops, new features are introduced, or legal and operational requirements change.
